λLAMBDAOS

Settings

Lambda OS reads everything with your own browser sign-ins. Nothing leaves your browser and no passwords or tokens are stored.

Appearance

Changes apply right away here, and on the next new tab. The map and second-brain stage stays dark in both themes so imagery and glow stay readable.

Data

Runs in the background even when no dashboard tab is open, and alerts are checked after each refresh. Open dashboards pick up new data once you've been idle for a minute. Faster refreshes mean more requests to Jira, NetBox and NetSuite.

Slack is read through your open Slack tab (app.slack.com), so keep one open. Gmail and Calendar use your Google sign-in in Chrome.

Jira

Ticket fields are matched by name: “Data Center Location(s)”, “Hostname”, “Cabinet Name”, “Rack Position”, “Lambda Part Number”, “Time to resolution”. Deployments use “Planned Start”, “Planned End”, “Completion Date”, “Deployment Type”, “Slack Channel”. Progress counts their child workstreams.

NetBox

Needs the VPN. “Low” stock means at or below each line’s alert quantity.

NetSuite

Read with your NetSuite sign-in, using each saved search’s CSV export. The ID is the number after searchid= in the search’s address.

Google
Alerts
Alerts are checked after every background refresh (every 10 minutes). The first check only takes a baseline, so you're not flooded.
Daily brief
Meetings, your breached and due tickets, tracked deployments with progress since yesterday, new tickets and low spares at your sites, parts arrived, mentions. With an AI provider set, the brief can also summarize itself.
AI assistant

Ask with ✨ Ask AI in the top bar or from the search bar: answers are grounded in the dashboard's loaded data and link straight to tickets, deployments and sites. Your key stays in this browser, is sent only to the provider you pick, and is never written to backups.

Privacy: what the AI may see

Masked values are swapped for placeholders like [PERSON_2] before the request leaves your browser, and swapped back in the answer only on your screen. Under every AI answer, What was sent shows the exact message.
